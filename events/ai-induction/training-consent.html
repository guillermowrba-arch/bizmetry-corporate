2. Scope

This policy applies to personal data collected through event registration forms, including LinkedIn event registration or lead forms, and any BizMetry-hosted registration pages related to the event.

3. Personal Data We Collect

Depending on the registration method, we may collect:

  • Identity & contact data: name, email address

We do not intentionally collect special category (sensitive) data. Please do not provide sensitive personal information through registration fields.

4. Purposes of Processing

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To deliver the event and provide follow-up materials (e.g., slides, recordings, resources)
  • To respond to inquiries and provide support related to the event
  • To improve our events and content (e.g., aggregate analytics, feedback)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, our legal bases for processing may include:

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your data only with:
