Inception in GenAI & Agentic AI — Training (for non-field experts)

Practical fundamentals for professionals who want clarity and a strong conceptual baseline.

Date: 03/06
Time: 12 PM PST | 5 PM ART
Duration: 2 hours

Overview

This session introduces core concepts behind Generative AI and Agentic AI, with a focus on practical understanding for professional audiences.

Agenda / Syllabus

  1. Introduction to Generative AI and Agentic AI
    • Definition and overview of Generative AI
    • What is Agentic AI?
  2. Key Concepts of GenAI
    • AI models and their training
    • Main learning paradigms: supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement learning, adversarial networks
  3. Transformer Architecture
    • Role of Transformers in modern AI systems
    • Basic architecture and functionality
    • Attention mechanism overview
  4. Embeddings and Cosine Distance
    • What embeddings are and why they are powerful
    • Vector representations of semantic meaning
    • Similarity calculation using cosine distance
  5. RAG and Content Pipeline
    • Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)
    • Vector databases and retrieval strategies
    • Data flow and integration processes
  6. LLM vs SLM Models
    • Differences between Small Language Models (SLMs) and Large Language Models (LLMs)
    • Performance, cost, and scalability trade-offs
    • Use cases, characteristics, and examples
  7. Agent Orchestration and Agentic Flow
    • Introduction to agent orchestration concepts
    • Agentic workflows and decision flows
    • Orchestration methods:
      • Sequential orchestration
      • LLM-driven orchestration
  8. MCP (Model Context Protocol)
    • Communication protocol between agents and tools
    • Context sharing and structured tool invocation
    • Example integrations with external systems
  9. A2A Protocol (Agent-to-Agent)
    • Standard communication protocol between agents
    • Message formats and collaboration patterns
    • How agents collaborate within an ecosystem
  10. Agentic Frameworks
    • Comparison of frameworks (Strands, CrewAI, OpenAI SDK)
    • Best choice depending on the use case
    • Agentic Reference Architecture overview
